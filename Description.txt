
Living Room
The small living room contains a couch, coffee table, and two
armchairs. Pictures of various cousins adorn the walls along
with two of your aunt's wretched attempts at oil painting. You
can't help but notice that the carpet could use a good
vacuuming.
Doorways lead west to your father's office, north to your
bedroom, and east to the kitchen.
#
Bedroom
This is your domain and has been since your family moved here
when you were six. A nightstand with a Superman lamp sits next
to your twin bed. Posters of various anime characters cover one
wall. A pile of laundry, slowly but surely attaining sentience,
occupies the far corner.
The door to the attic is in the ceiling above the pile of
clothes. You can return south to the living room.
#
Office
Your father's office smells like old books and strong coffee.
Papers cover a wooden desk, and a computer screen glows beside
a forgotten mug. A tall bookshelf leans against the wall.
One drawer is slightly open, as if someone left in a hurry.
The doorway leads east to the living room.
#
Kitchen
Sunlight shines through the kitchen window onto a crowded
counter. Dirty dishes fill the sink, and a refrigerator hums
in the corner. A plate of cookies sits on the table, but one
looks suspiciously like it has already been bitten.
A doorway leads west to the living room. Stairs lead down
to the basement.
#
Attic
Dust floats through the beam of light from a tiny window.
Cardboard boxes and old holiday decorations cover the floor.
A rocking horse stands beside a locked trunk. The wooden
boards creak beneath your feet, making every step sound loud.
The opening in the floor leads down to your bedroom.
#
Basement
The basement is cool and dim. A single light bulb hangs above
a workbench covered with rusty tools. The washing machine
rattles in the corner. Behind a stack of boxes, you hear a
quiet scratching sound.
The stairs lead up to the kitchen.
#
